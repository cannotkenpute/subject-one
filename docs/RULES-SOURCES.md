# Sources — Rules reference

This document records where the content of the **Rules reference** page (`src/lib/rules.ts`, served at
`#rules`) comes from, and how far it can be trusted. It is the counterpart to
[`public/data/SOURCES.md`](../public/data/SOURCES.md), which covers the question bank.

The two are independent: the question bank is a redistributed July 2022 community dataset, while the
rules reference was written for this project from published legal texts and official pages.

| Instrument | Used for |
|---|---|

| 《机动车驾驶证申领和使用规定》 — Ministry of Public Security Order No. 162 | Probation-period restrictions, licence classes (准驾车型), age conditions, validity and renewal |

## 3. How the content was compiled

1. The point lists were taken from the text of Order No. 163 and cross-checked item by item against a
   second independent summary of the same order. Only items that agreed across sources were included.
2. Each entry is written as a **bilingual pair**: an English study rendering and a Chinese line close
   to the source wording. Where the two differ in nuance, the Chinese is the authoritative one.
3. Entries are **summaries written for study, not verbatim law**. Article numbers are given in this
   document rather than in the app, to keep the page readable.

## 4. Known limits

- **Point-list verification, 14 October 2026.** The point-deduction groups were checked against Articles 8–12 of the [official text published by the Beijing Public Security Bureau](https://gaj.beijing.gov.cn/wsgs/2024zcwj/zcfg/202406/t20240628_3732202.html). The reference now has 50 study entries across the five levels. This check replaced an unsupported 6-point trailer entry, added the restricted dangerous-chemicals and school-bus inspection rules, and corrected vehicle scope, injury terminology and threshold wording. Some compound provisions are grouped or split for study. The point quiz uses these same entries. This verification does not extend to the other reference sections.
- **Point values only.** Fines, detention and licence suspension are covered for alcohol offences,
  where they are the main thing being tested, but not for every scored violation.
- **Local variation.** 学法减分 formats and credit amounts are set by provincial and municipal traffic
  authorities through the 交管12123 app. The app describes the national framework, not any one
  locality's implementation.
- **Rules change.** Everything here reflects the law as published at the dates above. Penalties and
  procedures are amended regularly.

## 5. Status

The Rules reference is **unofficial educational material**, prepared independently. It is not
affiliated with, endorsed by, or verified by the Ministry of Public Security (公安部), any Vehicle
Management Office (车管所), or any other authority. It is not legal advice. Confirm anything that
matters against current official sources before your exam and before driving.
